## Required capabilities - all present

| requirement | implementation |
|---|---|
| measure selector | dropdown populated from `/v2/mfs/measures` |
| financial-year selector | dropdown populated from `/v2/mfs/years?measure_type=...` |
| reporting-month selector | dropdown populated from the selected year's actual facts |
| flow/stock indicator | a coloured badge - "Flow (year-to-date)" or "Stock (point-in-time)", switching live when the measure selector changes |
| YTD-period disclosure | a badge reading e.g. "Year-to-date flow through May 2025-26 (month 11 of 12 - this series never reaches a 12th/June column, so it is never a full-year annual actual)" for flows, or "Point-in-time stock at 2026-05-31" for stocks |

| citation access | clicking a chart data point (year-over-year section) populates a citation panel with the real source locator + cached copy path |

## The three required visualizations, plus the fourth

1. **YTD revenue vs YTD expenses by reporting month** - `/v2/mfs/compare?measure_types=mfs_ytd_revenue,mfs_ytd_expense`,
   two distinct lines, never summed.
2. **Fiscal balance and underlying cash balance as separate lines** -
   `/v2/mfs/compare?measure_types=mfs_ytd_fiscal_balance,mfs_ytd_underlying_cash_balance`.
3. **Net debt, net worth, assets, liabilities as point-in-time stock
   series** - `/v2/mfs/compare?measure_types=mfs_stock_total_assets,mfs_stock_total_liabilities,mfs_stock_net_worth,mfs_stock_net_debt`.
4. **Latest report vs prior-year same-month comparison** - two `/v2/mfs/series`
   calls (selected year and a selectable prior year) for the currently
   selected measure, plotted as two lines aligned by reporting month
   (same elapsed fiscal period, never a partial-vs-full-year mismatch).

**No annual expenditure pie was built or touched** - this page has no
sunburst/pie chart at all, only line charts, and does not import or
render `SpendingChart.tsx`.
